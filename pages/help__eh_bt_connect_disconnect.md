# BT Connection

Source: https://tasker.joaoapps.com/userguide/en/help/eh_bt_connect_disconnect.html

Will trigger every time a bluetooth device connects **OR** disconnects from your Android device.

You can check which of the events happened (connection/disconnection) by checking the **%bt_connected** variable in the profile's task.

Check out an example of how to use this [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HmcNmtJvSvw).
