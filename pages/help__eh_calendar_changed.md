# Calendar Notification

Source: https://tasker.joaoapps.com/userguide/en/help/eh_calendar_changed.html

### Calendar Update Notification

This event is triggered whenever a change is detected in your device's calendar by Android.

Note: At times, event-specific changes may not be identified, resulting in no values for variables. This is often due to modifications in related data, such as reminders or attendees.
