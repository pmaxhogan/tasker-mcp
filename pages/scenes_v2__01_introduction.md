# Tasker Screen Builder Manual: Introduction

Source: https://tasker.joaoapps.com/userguide/en/scenes_v2.html#introduction

Section 1 of [Tasker Screen Builder Manual](scenes_v2) (Getting Started).

**Design custom screens for your Tasker projects - visually, without writing code.**

The Tasker Screen Builder lets you create beautiful, custom screens for your Tasker projects. Design input forms, dashboards, control panels, status displays - anything you can imagine - all from a visual editor, no coding required.

Layouts are built from components arranged in a tree. You pick containers (Column, Row, Box) to control arrangement, add content components (Text, Button, Image, etc.), and style everything with modifiers. Under the hood it's JSON, but you never need to touch it unless you want to.

Show your screens as fullscreen activities, floating dialogs, overlays on top of other apps, or even as a screensaver. Connect them to Tasker tasks to create fully interactive experiences.
