# Accessibility Services

Source: https://tasker.joaoapps.com/userguide/en/help/ah_accessibility_services.html

Allows you to control if accessibility services on your device are running or not.

[Watch a demo here](https://youtu.be/otQYsZhgpR0)

[Watch a demo of querying services here](https://youtu.be/KrRSP_a7bv4)

Actions:

- **Start**: will start any services in the **Services** field. All other services will keep their state
- **Stop**: will stop any services in the **Services** field. All other services will keep their state
- **Stop All**: will stop all accessibility services on your device.
- **Add To Keep Running**: will add the services in the **Services** to the **Keep Running Accessibility** setting available in Tasker Preferences.
- **Remove From Keep Running**: will remove the services in the **Services** from the **Keep Running Accessibility** setting available in Tasker Preferences.
- **Toggle**: if a service in the **Services** field is currently not running, it will start running. If it's already running, it will be stopped.
