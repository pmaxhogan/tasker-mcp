# Get Battery Info

Source: https://tasker.joaoapps.com/userguide/en/help/ah_get_battery_info.html

Gets various info about the battery on your device.

**Important**: not all info may be available on all devices.

You can use [this project](https://taskernet.com/shares/?user=AS35m8ne7oO4s%2BaDx%2FwlzjdFTfVMWstg1ay5AkpiNdrLoSXEZdFfw1IpXiyJCVLNW0yn&id=Project%3AShow+All+Local+Variables) to easily display a list of all the values this action produces.
