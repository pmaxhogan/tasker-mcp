# Logcat Entry

Source: https://tasker.joaoapps.com/userguide/en/help/eh_logcat_entry.html

Monitor your device's logcat for any relevant log lines

Use the built-in helper (magnifying glass) to quickly find out the logs you need to monitor.

To learn more about the **Grep Filter** option, [check here](https://github.com/joaomgcd/TaskerDocumentation/blob/master/en/help/logcat%20info.md).

[Check here for a video example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2YH0JBSdpNE).
