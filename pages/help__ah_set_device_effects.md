# Set Device Effects

Source: https://tasker.joaoapps.com/userguide/en/help/ah_set_device_effects.html

This action allows you to customize device effects such as grayscale, dim wallpaper, and disabling the always-on display (AOD). Adjust these settings to enhance visual comfort or preserve battery life on your device.

As Google continues to enhance Android with more features, if new device effects become available, they will also be added to Tasker.

Grayscale

Enable this to turn your device screen to grayscale, which can reduce eye strain and save battery.

Dim Wallpaper

Enable this to dim your device's wallpaper for a less distracting background and potentially lower battery consumption.

Disable Always-On Display (AOD)

Enable this to turn off the always-on display feature, which shows information on the screen even when it is off. Disabling AOD can help save battery.
